About this demo

A simple browser-based showcase of the FoodVerse nutrition explorer.

This hosted demo uses a small predefined dataset. It does not connect to Gemini or another AI service and does not identify food from an uploaded image. Nutrition values are approximate and vary by serving size and recipe.
